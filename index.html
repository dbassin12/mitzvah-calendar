Our why

“If you spend your whole day scheduling the mundane, you never spend a minute scheduling your Judaism.”

The Mitzvah Calendar started as a voice note between two friends. We noticed that everything ordinary in life gets a date on the wall — meetings, leases, little league — while the things we actually daven for stay vague. “This year I'll get to Eretz Yisrael. This year I'll finish Shas Mishnayos. This year, Uman.” A goal without a date is a wish.

So we made the year visible. One large sheet, every week of 5787, with the Yamim Tovim already shining on it — and room beside them to commit, in ink, to the mitzvos you'll actually do. Hang it where the family sees it. Watch a year of Yiddishkeit fill in.

01

The whole year, one view

All of 5787 lined up on a single sheet — and this year that means 13 months, because 5787 is a leap year with two Adars. 385 days at a glance.

02

Hebrew + English dates

Every day carries both calendars, so 15 Nissan and April 22 live on the same square — no more flipping between apps to plan a Yom Tov trip.

03

Yamim Tovim pre-marked

Every Yom Tov, fast day, and Rosh Chodesh is printed in gold before it ever reaches your wall. Shabbos columns are shaded the whole year through.

04

The parsha, every week

Each week is labeled with its parsha, so the calendar reads like the year actually moves — from Bereishis to V'Zos HaBracha.

05

Erev Shabbos cues

A candle-lighting reminder sits on every Friday, and a motzei-Shabbos mark closes every week. The rhythm of the week, printed in.

06

Room to commit

Generous write-in space for your own mitzvah goals: the Israel trip, the siyum, the chesed project. It's not a calendar of what happens — it's a calendar of what you'll make happen.

FAQ

When does it ship?

The first printing ships in Elul 5786 (late summer 2026), so it's on your wall before Rosh Hashanah. Pre-order reservations are fulfilled first, in order of signup.

How big is it, and what's it printed on?

24″ × 36″ — genuinely big — on thick matte stock that takes pencil, pen, and marker beautifully. It ships rolled in a protective tube, never folded.

Is it Hebrew, English, or both?

Both. Every date carries its Hebrew and English day side by side, month names appear in both languages, and the Yamim Tovim are labeled the way you actually say them.

Why 13 months?

5787 is a shanah me'uberes — a Jewish leap year — so there are two Adars and 385 days. The calendar shows the real Jewish year, exactly as it runs: Rosh Hashanah to Rosh Hashanah.

Do you take school or bulk orders?

Yes — that's a channel we care about deeply. See the schools section or email us with your quantity and we'll send bulk pricing.

Can I sponsor calendars for others?

Yes. Sponsors can underwrite calendars for a school or community, with a printed dedication line on the sponsored run. Details here.

What does it cost?

Founding pricing is announced when the first printing opens — and the pre-order list hears first, with a founders' discount. Reserving a spot costs nothing now.
