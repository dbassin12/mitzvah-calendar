Our Why

Our why

You schedule everything mundane.
Schedule your Judaism too.

The Mitzvah Calendar started as a voice note between two friends: every ordinary thing in life gets a date — meetings, leases, little league — while the things we actually daven for stay vague. “This year I'll get to Eretz Yisrael. This year I'll finish Mishnayos. This year, Uman.” A goal without a date is a wish.

So we made the year visible. One big sheet, every week of 5787, the Yamim Tovim already marked — and room beside them to commit, in ink, to the mitzvos you'll actually do. Hang it where the family sees it. Watch a year of Yiddishkeit fill in.

Questions

The fine print

When does it ship?

The first printing ships late summer 2026 — on your wall before Rosh Hashanah 5787. Reservations are fulfilled first, in order of signup.

How big is it?

24″ × 36″ — genuinely big — on thick matte stock that takes pencil, pen, and marker beautifully. Ships rolled in a tube, never folded.

Hebrew or English?

Both. Every square carries its Hebrew and English date, month names appear in both languages, and the Yamim Tovim are labeled the way you actually say them.

Why 13 months?

5787 is a shanah me'uberes — a leap year — so there are two Adars and 385 days. The calendar shows the real Jewish year, Rosh Hashanah to Rosh Hashanah.

School and bulk orders?

Yes — a channel we care about deeply. Email us your quantity and we'll send bulk pricing timed to the school year.

Can I sponsor calendars?

Yes. Sponsors underwrite calendars for a school or community, with a printed dedication line on the sponsored run. Details here.

What does it cost?

Founding pricing is announced when the first printing opens — the pre-order list hears first and gets the founders' discount. Reserving costs nothing.

Who's behind this?

Two friends who kept saying “this year, im yirtzeh Hashem” and decided the year needed a wall. Questions? Email us.
